The 5 Marketing Agents
Agent	Objective	Tools It Owns
📸 Photo Studio	"Make my vehicle photos look professional"	Background removal, background swap, studio image generation
🎬 Video Producer	"Turn my photos into video content"	Photo→video, voiceover generation, motion prompting

Each agent is completely isolated — its own system prompt, its own tool set, its own conversation thread, its own visor. But they all share the same Studio gallery as their output destination. That's the connective tissue.

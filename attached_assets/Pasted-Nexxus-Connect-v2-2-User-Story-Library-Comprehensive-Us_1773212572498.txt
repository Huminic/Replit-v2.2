Executive Summary
 
This document serves as the authoritative source of truth for the functional capabilities of Nexxus Connect v2.2. It is designed for dual utility: creating rigorous test cases for Quality Assurance (QA) and providing narrative-driven feature highlights for Marketing teams.
 
The "Dual-Narrative" approach adopted herein captures the complete lifecycle of an interaction. We do not view user actions in isolation; instead, we map the Prospect/Customer experience directly against the Internal Team handling. This ensures that every external touchpoint (Widget, Voice, SMS) corresponds to an operational workflow within the dealership.
 
Scope Coverage: This library details workflows for Sales and Service departments, covering all AI agents (Communication, Service, CRM Guru), the Trigger Engine, Campaigns, Two-way Messaging, and the critical VIN Solutions integration.

4. User Stories

 
 
A. Sales Lead Capture

[US-001]​Web Chat to VIN Lead Creation
 
 
As a Prospect
I want to inquire about a vehicle via the website chat widget So that I can get information quickly without a phone call
 
NARRATIVE
A prospect lands on the dealership landing page and engages the Universal Widget, selecting "Web Chat." They inquire about the availability of a 2024 SUV. The AI collects their name, phone number, and specific vehicle interest.
 
Simultaneously, Nexxus processes this interaction. A new lead is immediately posted to VIN Solutions via API with the source marked as "Nexxus Web Chat." The chat transcript is appended to the CRM notes.
 
A Salesperson receives a notification in TeamBox. They open the lead, view the transcript, and use the "Send SMS" feature to confirm availability, initiating a personal connection.
 
TECHNOLOGY STACK
Landing Page
Universal Widget (Web Chat) VIN Solutions API
TeamBox
 
DATA FLOW
Widget Input → Local Processing → VIN Solutions (Lead Create) → TeamBox (Notification)
 
SUCCESS CRITERIA
Lead appears in VIN Solutions within 60 seconds. Source is correctly attributed to "Nexxus Web Chat". Salesperson can view transcript in TeamBox.
